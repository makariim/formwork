# Personal defaults

For your own global instruction file (for Claude Code, `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`).
Copy the block, fill it in, delete what you do not need. It is yours. Formwork
never reads or writes it.

**Keep the project out of it.** What one project needs goes in that project's
`docs/standing.md` and `docs/routing.md`. This file is what holds in every
project: who you are, and how you like to work.

**One line about formwork, at most.** The last line of the block. Routing rules
live in the kit, so they change in one place.

---

```markdown
# How I work

## Who I am

<one or two lines: your role, what you know well, what you do not>

## Defaults

- <how work runs: for example, run alone inside a brief and collect choices for the end>
- <what to do when unsure: for example, ask one question, never pick the biggest size>
- <what never to do without asking>

## Taste

- <what good looks like to you, in your words>
- <what you always cut>

## How to talk to me

- <length, language, tone>
- <what you are reading for in a reply>

In a project with FORMWORK.md, follow its routing: `formwork/route.md`.
```
