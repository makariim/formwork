# Predictions

**The challenger writes this before anybody proposes anything.**

Save it as `docs/rounds/<name>/predictions.md`. A check looks for it, and
[`../round.md`](../round.md) says why the order matters.

## What would show me I was wrong

For each one above, what result would change your mind.

This is the part people skip, and skipping it is how a prediction becomes a
thing to argue about later instead of a thing to check.

## What I think this round will decide

Your guess at the outcome, before you have read anybody else's work.

Write it even when you are unsure. Especially then.

## What nobody has asked

The question you expect to be missing from every file in this round.

## How confident am I

Plainly. "Fairly sure" and "guessing" are both useful and honest. A number you
invented is not.
