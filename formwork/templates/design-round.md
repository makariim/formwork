# Design round

Optional. A brief for designing something the spec already describes: a
look, a set of screens, or an architecture. Give it `stream: design`.

---

```markdown
---
status: open
stream: design
starts: after spec
---

# NNNN Design: <what>

## Team
| Role | Focus |
|---|---|
| visual as art director | the look |
| ux | the journeys in the story |

## What to design, in story order
The parts of the spec, in the order the story meets them.

## Taste packs
The packs the samples are looked at with, from `formwork/taste/`, and the
project's design file, which wins: `Taste packs: screens, web`.
The three kit items in `formwork/taste/README.md` always apply.

## The sample stop
First a concept on one page and a few samples. Then **stop**, and give the
person the samples to pick from, with a recommendation. Nothing beyond the
samples is designed before the pick.

## For an architecture
Two or three whole designs, each walked through every scenario in the spec.
The walk is the comparison: where each design breaks, and what it costs.

## Done when
The person has picked, in their words, and the design is written down in
one document the build can follow.
```

---

**Why the stop.** A design built out in full before anybody chose a
direction is the most expensive thing to throw away. Samples first is cheap.
