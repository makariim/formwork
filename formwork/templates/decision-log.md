# Decision log

Optional. The director's running record of rulings while lanes work, numbered,
each with its evidence. Lighter than a decision in `docs/decisions/`; a ruling
that changes the project's direction still becomes one, written by the human.
The method is in [`../operations/README.md`](../operations/README.md).

Saved as `docs/decision-log.md`, or one per round beside the round's files.
Newest last. A ruling is never edited; a later one revises it and says so.

---

```markdown
# Decision log: <project or round>

Every ruling the director made, with the evidence that prompted it. Newest
last. A ruling that overrides a registered bar says "deviation".

| # | Date | Ruling | Why (evidence) | Revises |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | YYYY-MM-DD | what was decided, plainly | the report and section, the measured number with its command, or the human's words | |
```
