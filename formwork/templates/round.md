# Round record

What comes out of several agents arguing about one question.

A file in the repository, not a chat summary. You will read it months later.

---

```markdown
---
question: the one thing this round was called to settle
date: 2026-09-12
lead: who ran it
participants: [who argued]
---

# Round 004. The question in one line

## What is proposed, and who proposed it

Every part attributed to whoever put it forward.

Attribution is not politeness. Being able to follow any position back to its holder, and to the grounds they
held it on, is how you come to read the team.

## Where we disagreed

Per disagreement: the argument on each side, the evidence each of them carried,
the resolution, and **whether whoever lost was convinced or simply overruled.**

Where a participant shifted position, record what shifted it.

**An empty section here means the round failed.** Agreement with no friction is
the normal failure, not the normal success. Say what you did about it.

## What we turned down, and why

This is the section that stops a future round proposing the same thing again.

## Still open, for you

What the team could not settle, or should not have. Do not resolve these to
look decisive.

For each: the question, both positions, and what changes depending on the
answer.

## What this costs

- What it makes harder.
- What it rules out later.
- What fails quietly.
- Who maintains it in six months, and what they will have forgotten.

## What the round itself cost

Money, if you can read it from the tool. Time. How many exchanges.

Never estimate a figure you were unable to observe. State which quantities you
counted, and which stayed out of reach.
```

---

## Before the round starts

The challenger writes its predictions **first**, before reading anybody else's
work. What each participant will propose, where each will be weak, and what
the group will settle on without being pushed.

Afterwards it records what it got right and what it got wrong.

Reading somebody's work shapes any objection composed afterwards. Predictions
escape that. One that turns out accurate shows the weakness was built in rather
than accidental, which is a much stronger result.

This ordering is checkable and will be checked.
