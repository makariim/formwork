## Formwork's change to §19

This section is not from upstream. Where it and §19 disagree, this section wins.

Remove decorative bold and bold labels, as §19 says. When the text is long
enough to have sections, keep one bold phrase in each section, on the thing
that matters most in it. If a section has several, keep the one on its main
point and remove the rest. Do not add bold the writer did not use. Text without
sections follows §19 as written.

In step 3, one bold phrase per section is not a tell.
