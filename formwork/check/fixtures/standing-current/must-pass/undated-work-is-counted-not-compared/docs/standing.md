# Standing brief

## What we are building

A small tool that turns a folder of photographs into a printable contact sheet.

## Where we are now

The spec is written. Research on the printing side has started.

## What is decided

- One page size only, for now.

## What is open

Whether to support a second page size.

## What is next

Make the page size a setting.

## What we tried and stopped

Reading the size from the printer. Too many ways for it to be missing.
