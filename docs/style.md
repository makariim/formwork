# How to talk to me

Live, not history. This is the project's own preference, and it beats
`formwork/style.md` wherever the two disagree.

**Plain simple English.** Simple words, simple sentences, simple grammar.
English is not my first language, and neither is it for many people who will
read this repository.

**Short.** If it can be said in three lines, do not write ten. I will ask when
I want more.

**No em dashes.** No dashes standing in for a pause. Use a full stop or a
comma.

**Nothing that sounds like it was written to impress.** No clever phrasing, no
long words where a short one works, no jokes that need explaining.

**Say what changed and what happens next.** Those two are what I am reading
for.

**Do not lead with numbers.** Tell me what the thing is and what it is made of.
The counts come after, with the command that produced them.

**Ask me one thing at a time.**
