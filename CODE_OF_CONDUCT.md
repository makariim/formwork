# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people. Attack the work, never the person.

## What that means here

**Criticism of the kit is welcome and wanted.** Tell it that a guard is wrong,
a rule is useless, a number is made up. That is the whole point of this
project, and the log of it doing exactly that to its own author is in
[docs/dogfood.md](docs/dogfood.md).

**Criticism of a person is not.** Insults, harassment, and personal attacks are
not welcome in issues, pull requests or anywhere else.

## If something goes wrong

Open an issue, or write to the maintainer through GitHub.

There is one maintainer. He will read it, and he will say what he decided and
why.
